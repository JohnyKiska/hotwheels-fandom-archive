## What is deliberately not done

- No casting, variant, release, or assortment identities are created from source assertions alone.
- No Gallery image is automatically linked to a product.
- Group-level evidence does not prove a specific row-to-image or car-to-package identity.
- `Image Not Available` placeholders remain source values, not image assets.
- Prototype, package, set, sample, and advertising imagery remain evidence until a reviewer resolves the subject.
- Cross-checked group evidence with `probable` confidence is not promoted to 100% verified canonical data.
