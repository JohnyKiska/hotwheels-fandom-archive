# Pro Racing 1:43 release and assortment candidate review

Status: staged source interpretation only. No canonical Release or Assortment entities have been created.

## Scope and method

- 41 product-table rows are retained as 41 distinct source release candidates. A source row is not yet proof of a unique retail release.
- Variant candidate IDs are carried forward from the preceding row-level review, including holds.
- Explicit pack notes are modeled separately from the car's Variant: a multi-pack is an Assortment containing component release candidates, not one new Casting or Variant.
- Blank packaging fields remain unknown. They are not filled by inference from Gallery images or filename text.

- 11 rows assigned to four named provisional assortment groups: World Champions 2-Pack (2), Constructors Champions 4 Pack (4), Hot Wheels Racing B7022 3-Pack (3), and Hot Wheels Racing B6223 Ferrari F2004 Constructors Champions 2-Pack (2).
- 22 rows held because release/packaging details are absent.

## Assortment handling

- Rows 17-18: one World Champions 2-Pack candidate with two distinct component cars. The set name is explicit; component-to-variant details remain tied to the source rows.
- Rows 26-29: one Constructors Champions 4 Pack candidate. The four annual Ferrari model rows are its contents; Gallery review explicitly says the single tray image is set-level evidence, not four individual car photographs.
- Rows 30-32: three rows say “Part of a 3 Pack”. External catalog evidence identifies Hot Wheels Racing item B7022 as a 1:43 set containing Ferrari F1-2000 #3 Malaysia GP, Ferrari F2001 #1 Belgian GP, and Ferrari F2002 #1 Canadian GP. The three casting labels align exactly with source rows 30-32, so these rows are now grouped under a **B7022 candidate assortment**. This is a probable source-to-assortment match, not proof that the Terry/sample source rows depict those exact cars or that the source pack is the retail B7022 package. Keep the assortment staged; do not promote it to canonical.
- Rows 38-39: both say “Comes in a 2 Pack” and carry complementary F2004 numbers #1 and #2. Independent 1:43 Hot Wheels records identify B6223 / EAN 0027084044867 as the 2004 Ferrari Constructors Championship two-car set with Michael Schumacher #1 and Rubens Barrichello #2. The distinctive Ferrari-shield presentation case and package photos are documented. This is a strong B6223 candidate assortment match, but no Terry Choy source-row package photo proves the exact source-to-retail package link. Keep staged and non-canonical.

## External cross-check: Ferrari six-car World Champions display (Gallery group G17)

A separate collector listing provides external evidence for the **six-car** Ferrari display shown in Gallery items 39–52. The ended eBay listing is titled “RARE Hot Wheels Formula 1 Ferrari 6 in-a-row World Constructors' Championships” and describes six red Ferrari F1 cars on a black stand with gold “Hungaroring 2004” and “World Constructors' Championships” text. Its item specifics identify the model years as F1-1999, F2000, F2001, F2002, F2003 and F2004. This independently supports the existence of a physical six-car display corresponding closely to the Terry Choy Gallery subject. It does not supply a Mattel item number, barcode, production quantity or manufacturer catalog record; treat it as a strong collector-market corroboration, not definitive proof of a standard retail release.

Do **not** conflate this six-car display with Hot Wheels **L6237**. Independent wholesaler listings describe L6237 as a separate 1:43 Ferrari Michael Schumacher Championship Collection containing **five** cars: F1-2000, F2001, F2002, F2003-GA and F2004. The 1999 F399 is absent. L6237 is therefore not an established SKU for the six-car G17 display.

**Disposition:** retain G17 as one set-level candidate with six annual content references and 14 Gallery evidence images. Add the external listing as corroboration for the physical six-car display, but keep the exact release identity / item code unresolved. Do not assign L6237 to G17, do not create six individual Gallery-to-car image relations, and do not promote a canonical Assortment yet.
