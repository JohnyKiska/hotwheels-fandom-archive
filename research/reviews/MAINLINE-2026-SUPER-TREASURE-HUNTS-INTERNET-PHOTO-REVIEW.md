## Catalog conflicts

### '87 Ford Sierra Cosworth JJM19: #116 vs #119 / unclear marketplace image

The archive's Mainline extraction, Fandom, Wheel Wonderland, HWC Garage and several specialist catalogs identify JJM19 as collector #116. HWtreasure and Collects.in list #119. The marketplace image preview for one carded listing is also difficult to read and may show a different number. Do not promote the photo's collector-number reading as verified. Keep #116 as the working value because it is corroborated by the Mainline source and several specialist references; retain the #119 conflict.

### Honda Civic Custom JJM20: #159 vs #151

The carded image visibly shows 159/250. Fandom and HWC Garage also list #159. Some list pages, including one 164Custom result, give #151. Retain #159; the card photo and multiple catalog references support it.

### Lotus Sport Elise / Subaru Impreza: JJM22 vs JJM23

One STH checklist appears to swap the two toy codes. The better-supported assignment is:
- JJM22 — Lotus Sport Elise, #160, case H.
- JJM23 — Subaru Impreza WRX, #176, case J.

This pairing is supported by the Hot Wheels Collectors Newsletter, HWC Garage, Vanto and the case-specific photo results. Keep the conflicting checklist as a source discrepancy, not as an alternate canonical mapping.
