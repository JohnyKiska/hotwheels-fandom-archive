## Treasure Hunt card emblem: important color distinction

A regular Treasure Hunt is not expected to carry the gold circle-flame emblem associated with a Super Treasure Hunt. Collector references describe a **silver** circle-flame low-production mark on the regular TH card, while the STH card uses a **gold** circle-flame emblem.

The user's physical observations for JJM01 and JJM05 establish that the cars themselves carry differently colored flame marks (blue on the Viper, black on the Ford GT Race) and that no separate **gold** round TH logo was seen on either card. The available internet card photos do not consistently resolve the small card area well enough to establish whether a silver circle-flame is present or absent across the entire 2026 TH run. Do not generalize the two physical samples to all fifteen cards.
