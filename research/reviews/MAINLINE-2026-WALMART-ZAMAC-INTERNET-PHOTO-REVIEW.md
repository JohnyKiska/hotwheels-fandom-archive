## Catalog conflicts and corrections

### 1985 Honda CR-X: JJM40 vs JJZ05

The archive's Mainline extraction, Hot Wheels Collectors Newsletter and Fandom list the Zamac CR-X as **JJM40**. 164Custom's 2026 ZAMAC list instead gives **JJZ05**. The individual blister photo confirms the model and its #089/250 + HW J-Imports 4/10 packaging, but the toy number is not legible in the captured image. Retain JJM40 provisionally and preserve the source conflict; do not silently normalize.

References:
- [Hot Wheels Collectors Newsletter](https://catalog.hwcollectorsnews.com/seriesdefinitions/details/2279)
- [Fandom Zamac list](https://hotwheels.fandom.com/wiki/Walmart_Exclusive_Zamac_Editions)
- [164Custom Zamac list](https://164custom.com/hot-wheels/ZAMAC/2026.html)

### 1972 Datsun Sunny Coupe: collector #211 vs #213

The individual sealed-card photo visibly shows **211/250**. This supports collector #211, as listed by 164Custom and the archive's Mainline extraction. The Newsletter's Zamac-series page gives #213, which conflicts with its own product-family information and the carded photo. Retain #211; note #213 as a source discrepancy.

References:
- [Carded listing photo](https://www.ebay.com/itm/227531438289)
- [164Custom Zamac list](https://164custom.com/hot-wheels/ZAMAC/2026.html)
- [Newsletter series page](https://catalog.hwcollectorsnews.com/seriesdefinitions/details/2279)
