## Toy-number discrepancy: Porsche

One Hot Wheels Newsletter catalog page lists Porsche 911 Turbo Cabriolet as **JJM65**, and also lists Pontiac Firebird as JJM65. This is a duplicate-code conflict within that catalog.

The Porsche assignment **JJM67** is corroborated by the 164Custom Target Red Edition list, Fandom's 2026 list and Porsche casting page, and HW Collectors Garage. The Pontiac Firebird assignment **JJM65** is independently corroborated by the HW Collectors Garage vehicle page and other catalog sources. Retain JJM67 for Porsche and JJM65 for Firebird; record the Newsletter entry as a source discrepancy, not as a second Porsche code.
