## Distribution and case notes

- 164Custom's Kroger list places JJM49–JJM52 in case C and JJM53–JJM56 in case L. These case letters are retained as source-reported assortment information, not treated as proof of packaging differences.
- The four-car listing identifies its contents as Charger Daytona, Lamborghini Centenario Roadster, Rodger Dodger and Mustang Shelby GT500. The photograph shows all four sealed blister packs together.
- JJM53 and JJM54 have individual sealed-card photographs from marketplace listings.
- JJM55 and JJM56 remain incomplete for packaging review. Do not infer their blister/card details from catalog renders or loose-car images.
