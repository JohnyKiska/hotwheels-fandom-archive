## Interpretation notes

- 1996 Model Series #440 is a distinct source row and sits outside the main Collector # sequence; do not renumber it.
- Bonus Cars use Collector #000 and a separate Series # sequence (01/04–04/04); keep that scope separate from annual Mainline Collector #.
- The 1996 Hot Wheels table uses five columns and has no Series # field. This is a source schema difference, not missing data.
- Preserve raw Series # strings, including zero-width Unicode, and normalize only in derived fields.
- Repeated Collector # rows must remain distinct source rows until their release/color meaning is confirmed.
- Fandom is a secondary source and does not independently establish Mattel release status or casting/tooling identity.
